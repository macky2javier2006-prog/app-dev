# My Favorite Shows

Here are two of my favorite series: **Breaking Bad** and **Better Call Saul**.

---

## 1. Breaking Bad
* **Genre:** Crime / Drama
* **Main Character:** *Walter White*
* **Plot:** A chemistry teacher starts making blue meth to support his family.

> *"I am the danger."* - Walter White

---

## 2. Better Call Saul
* **Genre:** Legal Drama
* **Main Character:** *Jimmy McGill (Saul Goodman)*
* **Plot:** Shows how a small-time lawyer becomes the famous criminal attorney Saul Goodman.

> *"S'all good, man!"* - Jimmy McGill
